Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Tokkenly.

What is Tokkenly?

Tokkenly is an AI-powered global financial platform that brings digital dollar banking, international payments, everyday spending, savings and investments together in one account.

Who is Tokkenly for?

Tokkenly is for people who earn in dollars or crypto and want to do more with their money, as well as people who earn in local currency and want access to digital dollar savings, global investments and international spending opportunities.

Is Tokkenly a bank?

No. Tokkenly is a financial technology company. Payments and currency conversion are processed by licensed partners.

How do I fund my account?

Fund with naira, receive dollars through your US account details or deposit supported crypto assets from an external wallet.

Can I use Tokkenly if I only earn in naira?

Yes. You can convert your naira into digital dollars, save, earn on eligible balances, invest in stocks and spend internationally.

Can I receive international payments?

Yes. Receive supported dollar payments from clients, employers and platforms through your own US account details.

Can I convert dollars or crypto to naira?

Yes. Convert your balance to naira and withdraw to a supported local bank account. You'll see applicable fees and exchange rates before confirming.

How is my money held?

Your dollar balance is held as USDC, a digital asset designed to track the value of the US dollar. Digital assets carry risks. Learn more on our “How we hold your money” page.

Who controls my wallet?

You do. Tokkenly uses self-custodial wallet infrastructure and does not control your private keys. Our financial partners facilitate services such as payments and currency conversion.

What happens to my assets if Tokkenly shuts down?

Your self-custodial wallet assets remain on the blockchain and are not held by Tokkenly. Access to those assets depends on retaining access to your wallet keys or recovery credentials.

How do I back up my wallet?

Go to Settings › Security and privacy › Your keys and tap Export my key. Your key opens in a secure window. Tokkenly never receives it, and no one from Tokkenly will ever ask you for it. Store it somewhere private and safe: anyone who has your key can access your wallet. Without a backup, losing access to your email could mean losing access to your wallet.

Can I earn on my dollar balance?

Yes. Eligible dollar balances can earn returns. Visit our savings page for current rates, the source of returns and associated risks.

Can I invest with naira?

Yes. Fund your Tokkenly account with naira, convert it into digital dollars and invest in available global and local stocks.

Can I borrow against my investments?

Yes. You can borrow against eligible investments without having to sell them upfront. Borrowing is subject to collateral requirements and applicable terms. If your investments fall in value, your collateral may be liquidated.

What can Tokkenly AI do?

Tokkenly AI helps you understand your spending, track your investments and gain personalized financial insights through natural conversations about your money.

Do I need to understand crypto?

No. The underlying technology works behind the scenes, giving you a familiar and simple financial experience.

Where is Tokkenly available?

We're building for a global audience, starting with Nigeria. Product availability depends on your country of residence.
