What is Tokkenly?

Tokkenly is one account to send, receive, spend, save and invest in dollars. Fund it with your local currency or crypto, and use it like any money app.

Is Tokkenly a bank?

No. Tokkenly is a financial technology company. Licensed partners process payments and currency conversion.

How are my dollars held?

Your balance is held in USDC, a digital dollar designed to stay worth one US dollar. In the app, you just see dollars.

Do I need to understand crypto?

No. You see your balance in dollars and use Tokkenly like any money app. The technology works in the background.

How do I add money?

Send money by bank transfer in your local currency, or deposit crypto from your wallet or an exchange. Either way, it lands in your account as dollars.

Can I get paid from abroad?

Yes. You get your own US account and routing number, so clients, employers and platforms can pay you by ACH or wire.

Are the stocks real?

Tokkenly offers tokenized stocks that track the price of the real shares. Investments involve risk and can lose value.

What does Tokkenly AI do?

It answers questions about your own account in everyday words. It is read-only and can never move your money.

What is the minimum to start?

You can start with $1.
