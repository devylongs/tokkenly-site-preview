Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Tokkenly.

What is Tokkenly?

Tokkenly is one account to send, receive, spend, save and invest in dollars. Fund it with your local currency or crypto, and use it like any money app.

Is Tokkenly a bank?

No. Tokkenly is a financial technology company. Licensed partners process payments and currency conversion.

How is my money held?

Your dollar balance is held as USDC, a fully backed digital dollar designed to stay worth one US dollar. In the app, you just see dollars.

How do I fund my account?

Send money by bank transfer in your local currency, or deposit crypto from your wallet or an exchange. Either way, it lands in your account as dollars.

Can I receive international payments?

Yes. You get your own US account details, so clients, employers and platforms can pay you by ACH or wire.

Do I need to understand crypto?

No. You see your balance in dollars and use Tokkenly like any money app. The technology works in the background.

Where is Tokkenly available?

Tokkenly is available in select countries and expanding. Sign up to check if it's available where you are.
